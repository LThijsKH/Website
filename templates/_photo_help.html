How to navigate

Click a photo to open it with the camera, lens and settings it was taken with. Click it again to see it on its own.

On a photo

[ ← → ] [ A D ]
Previous or next photo
[ F ]
Photo on its own, or back to the details
[ Esc ]
Go back a step, or close this

On a touch screen, use the arrows beside the photo.
